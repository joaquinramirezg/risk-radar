An earlier question: What changed in Nvidia's export control risk this year?

Nvidia's export control risk was rewritten this year, and the new text is much more specific about the damage. The filing now says that "as of the end of fiscal year 2026, we were effectively foreclosed from competing in China's data center computing/compute market" 1. It also describes a licensing program for H200 chips into China, with any H200 shipped under it "subject to a 25% tariff upon importation into the United States" 2. Other additions warn that the government could extend controls to networking products 3, that customers may "design-out" U.S. chips 4, and that the H20 product left Nvidia with "excess inventory and purchase obligations" 5. The tool shows the section was rewritten but does not mark each sentence as new, so I can't say for certain which lines are entirely new.
